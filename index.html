Butter Burgers

NinetyFive starts from a real American story:
the Wisconsin butter burger.

Born around Milwaukee in the 1930s, it was about doing a few things right: fresh prime ground beef, butter-caramelised onions, soft butter and a warm potato bun.

At NinetyFive, we keep that idea simple and faithful.
No lettuce, no tomato, no unnecessary layers.

Authentic butter. Unmistakably better.
